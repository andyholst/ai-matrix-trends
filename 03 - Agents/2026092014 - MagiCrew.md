# MagiCrew

Open-source AI agent platform for deploying specialized digital workers. Product Hunt launch Sept 3, 2026.

## Key Facts

- **Website:** https://www.magicrew.ai
- **Product Hunt launch:** 2026-09-03 (3rd product of the day, 269 upvotes)
- **Founders:** Enzy Tian (ex-UN journalist), Rohan Chaubey, and others from business school
- **Category:** AI Infrastructure Tools / AI Workflow Automation

## Architecture

- Multi-agent collaboration platform with specialized agents for:
  - Research, data analysis, reports, presentations
  - Meetings, social media, creative work
- Enterprise controls, deliverable-ready outputs
- Self-hostable, open-source
- "The Costco of AI" — curated specialists, not wrappers or prompt templates
- Agents remember context and build on previous work

## Differentiation

- Not a coding agent per se — a general workforce platform
- "The longer you work inside MagiCrew, the less you have to explain"
- Focus on closing gap between what people can think and what they can get done
- Open-source and self-hostable (community demand per Reddit comments)
